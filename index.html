Frequently Asked Questions

Why a Personal Anniversary Wish Beats a Generic One

Anyone can copy a line from a card rack. What makes a wish land is the part only you could write: the memory, the inside joke, the quality you quietly admire. When you name the milestone and add one true detail, a short message becomes the thing they screenshot and keep. Our generator gives you a strong starting point; your personal touch is what turns it into a keepsake.

Three ways to personalize any wish

  • Open with the milestone. "Ten years" or "our first anniversary" sets the moment instantly.
  • Drop in one memory. The trip, the hard year you beat together, the tiny habit you love.
  • End on the feeling. "Still my favorite person" says more than any flourish.
What is the best anniversary wish?

The best wishes are personal. Mention a memory, name the milestone, and speak from the heart. A short, sincere message beats a long generic one every time.

Should I write a funny or romantic anniversary wish?

It depends on the relationship and the couple's style. For your partner, romantic usually lands best; for friends or colleagues, funny works great. When in doubt, warm and sincere never fails.

What do you write for a 25th or 50th anniversary?

Name the milestone — silver for 25, golden for 50 — and acknowledge the achievement: "25 years of love is a beautiful thing. Congratulations!"

Is it okay to send an anniversary message by text?

Absolutely. A heartfelt text means just as much as a card, especially for friends and family. For your partner, consider adding a handwritten note for extra romance.
