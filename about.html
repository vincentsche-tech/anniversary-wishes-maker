About Anniversary Wishes

Anniversary Wishes started with a simple problem: every year, we forget what to write. The anniversary card sits blank, the cursor blinks, and the words just won't come.

We built this site to fix that — so no one has to stare at a blank card when they want to celebrate the person they love.

What We Do

We write and carefully curate anniversary wishes for every relationship — husbands, wives, couples, parents, friends — and every milestone, from the first anniversary to the golden 50th. Every message is written with warmth and reviewed for sincerity.

Our Approach

  • Personal over generic. We give you a strong starting point and show you how to make it yours.
  • Every relationship, every milestone. The words for a wife are different from the words for parents, and we treat them that way.
  • Free forever. Finding the right words should never cost money.
